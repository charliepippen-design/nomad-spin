## What changed

The UK has approved an increase to the Student visa maintenance funds requirement, effective **30 November 2026**. Under the Immigration Rules change, applicants studying in London will need to show **£1,570** per month, replacing **£1,529**; those studying outside London will need **£1,203** per month, replacing **£1,171**. The required amount remains capped at nine months of course costs, and the changes apply to applications made on or after 30 November.

## Who it affects

This change affects international students applying for UK Student permission who must provide evidence of living costs. It also matters to people already abroad planning a course start in the UK, and to families budgeting for the move. The sums are a visa financial-evidence requirement—not a promise that the amount will cover actual rent and living expenses.

Some applicants may not need to submit financial evidence upfront, including certain nationals and people applying to extend after living in the UK with valid permission for at least 12 months. The underlying financial requirement may still apply, and UK Visas and Immigration may ask for evidence. Check whether an exemption applies to your circumstances rather than assuming nationality or current UK residence removes the requirement.

## When it applies

The change is approved but, as of 27 November 2026, is **not yet in force**. The Home Office’s Statement of Changes says the relevant Student rules (APP ST8 to APP ST10) take effect on **30 November 2026**. Applications made before that date are to be decided under the rules in force on 29 November; applications made on or after it fall under the new amounts. This is a change to required funds, not to course fees or the separate financial-evidence rules such as holding funds for the required period.

The public GOV.UK financial-evidence page still displays the previous monthly figures when checked on 27 November 2026. The new figures above come from the approved Immigration Rules text, so applicants should recheck the official guidance and application instructions after the effective date. The Rules do not change the nine-month cap in the cited amendments.

## What to do now

- If applying before 30 November, check which rules apply based on your actual application date and use the official current instructions.
- If applying on or after 30 November, budget for the new monthly figure for your study location, up to the applicable nine-month cap, in addition to course fees.
- Verify how dependants, exemptions, sponsorship, or funds already paid to your provider affect your evidence using current Home Office guidance.
- Keep evidence in the required format and check the latest instructions before submitting; do not rely on an older checklist.

This article is general information, not legal advice. Confirm the current rules with the official source or a qualified immigration adviser before acting.
